# HR Attrition Analysis — Power BI

## Project Overview

An interactive **Power BI HR Attrition Analysis Report** built using the IBM HR Employee Attrition dataset. The project analyzes employee demographics, attrition patterns, job characteristics, compensation, satisfaction, overtime, and other workforce factors to understand employee turnover.


* **Source:** IBM HR Employee Attrition dataset
* **Records:** 1,470 employees
* **Columns:** 35

## Dashboard 1 — HR Workforce and Attrition Overview

Provides a high-level overview of the organization's workforce and attrition.

### KPIs

* Total Employees
* Employees Left
* Attrition Rate
* Average Monthly Income
* Average Job Satisfaction

### Visual Analysis

* Employee Count by Job Role
* Attrition by Department
* Attrition by Age Group
* Attrition by Business Travel
* Gender Distribution
* Attrition rate by Marital Status

### Filters

* Department
* Gender
* Job Role
* Business Travel
* OverTime

## Dashboard 2 — Employee Retention & Attrition Drivers

Focuses on identifying employee and workplace factors associated with attrition.

### KPIs

* Attrition Rate
* Employees Retained
* Overtime Attrition Rate
* Average Tenure of Employees Who Left
* Average Income of Employees Who Left

### Visual Analysis

* Attrition by OverTime
* Attrition by Job Role and Job Satisfaction
* Attrition by Tenure Band
* Attrition by Income Band
* Attrition by Distance Band
* Attrition by Job Level

### Filters

* Department
* Job Role
* Job Level
* OverTime
* Business Travel

## Data Preparation

Data cleaning and transformation were performed using **Power Query**, including:

* Data-type validation
* Removal of unnecessary columns
* Removing null and duplicate values
* Creation of analytical groups/bands
* Preparation of fields for KPI and visual analysis

To convert employee-level HR data into an interactive analytical report that helps identify **attrition patterns and the employee, job, and workplace factors associated with employee turnover**.
